# Talkfolio

**Interactive CVs recruiters can question.**

[Visit Talkfolio](https://www.talkfolio.io)

Talkfolio is an evidence-based professional profile platform that helps candidates communicate their experience and recruiters understand it through AI-powered conversations.

### For candidates
Present your experience, responsibilities, contributions, and supporting evidence in an interactive professional profile.

### For recruiters
Ask questions about professional experience and receive AI-assisted answers grounded in available information, with uncertainty and limitations made explicit.

### Our principles
- Evidence over unsupported claims
- Transparency about professional contributions
- Clear boundaries for AI-generated answers
- Human judgment in hiring decisions
